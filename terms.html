1. What the tool does

Paste-Safe looks for secrets and personal details in text using patterns, replaces them with labels, and puts the real values back into an answer you paste. It runs entirely in your browser. See the privacy notice for how information is handled.

2. It can miss things

Detection is pattern-based and is not complete. It does not detect names of people or companies unless you add them to your word list, and it may miss new or unusual secret formats.
